# ARKON Engineering

This team performs approved product work across ARKON Platform and vertical execution repositories.

The Platform Architect owns technical boundaries. The Repo Harvest Engineer inventories existing behavior. Implementation engineers build approved changes. QA and security reviewers independently verify the result. Carl coordinates work with Nathan and controls escalation to the board.
